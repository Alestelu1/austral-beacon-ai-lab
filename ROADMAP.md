# Austral Beacon AI Lab — Roadmap 2026

This roadmap organizes the learning, technical development and project strategy for Austral Beacon AI Lab during the Alura ONE AI / AI for Tech path, CódigoFacilito Agents/Kiro course, and the transition from ChatGPT Plus/Codex to Gemini + Antigravity.

## Strategic Objective

Build reusable AI, automation, RAG and agentic infrastructure for the Austral Beacon ecosystem:

- Austral Beacon Media Network
- End of the World Atlas
- End of the World Travel
- Fin del Mundo Travel
- Antarctic Pulse
- Pulso Antártico
- Antarctica Begins
- Austral Dispatch

The goal is not only to complete courses, but to convert each learning module into useful infrastructure for southern / Antarctic media, geography, cartography, tourism, editorial intelligence and documentary storytelling.

### Every Course Session

After each Alura or CódigoFacilito class:

4. Update GitHub documentation.

## Strategic Rules

1. Build small reusable blocks.
2. Avoid overbuilding.
3. Do not create too many disconnected projects.
4. Keep Austral Beacon as the technical/media infrastructure layer.
5. Keep End of the World Atlas as the cartographic/documentary product.
6. Keep Antarctic Pulse as the monitoring/news intelligence product.
7. Use automation to assist editorial work, not replace verification.
8. Always preserve sources.
9. Always separate drafts from published content.
10. Always back up important project knowledge outside chat.
