## Editorial Principles

### Chilean Source Priority

When documenting Patagonia, Tierra del Fuego, Puerto Williams,
Canal Beagle, Cabo de Hornos, Antarctic gateways, Antarctic logistics,
and southern Chilean geography:

Priority order:

1. Chilean official sources
   - INE
   - INACH
   - SHOA
   - Armada de Chile
   - DGAC
   - CONAF
   - Gobierno Regional de Magallanes
   - Municipalidad de Cabo de Hornos

2. Scientific and international organizations
   - COMNAP
   - SCAR
   - Antarctic Treaty System
   - Peer-reviewed research

3. International media

Avoid repeating simplified international claims without verification
from Chilean primary sources.

### Example

Incorrect:

Puerto Williams is the southernmost settlement in the world.

Preferred:

Puerto Williams is the southernmost city in the world, officially
recognized as a city by the Chilean National Statistics Institute (INE)
in 2019.

Puerto Toro is located farther south but is classified as a hamlet
(caserío), not a city.

### Editorial Mission

Austral Beacon and End of the World Atlas aim to document southern Chile,
Patagonia, Tierra del Fuego, Cape Horn and Antarctic gateways through
verified geographic, scientific and historical sources.

The project prioritizes accuracy, cartographic context and primary
sources over simplified narratives frequently repeated in international
travel media.
