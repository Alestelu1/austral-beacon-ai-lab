# RAG Test Questions — End of the World Atlas

## Purpose

This document defines the first validation questions for the End of the World Atlas Knowledge Base.

These questions are designed to test:

- entity retrieval
- relationship tracing
- parent/child hierarchy
- gateway logic
- maritime route reasoning
- Antarctic logistics context

## Initial Questions

1. What is the logistical importance of Puerto Williams as a gateway compared with Punta Arenas?
2. Which geographic entities would a vessel encounter when navigating from Puerto Williams toward the Antarctic Peninsula?
3. How is Villa Las Estrellas related to Base Presidente Frei, and why is it significant within Bahía Fildes?
4. Which geographic landmarks define the oceanic transition zone around Cape Horn?
5. What infrastructure or services define an entity as a gateway in the Austral Beacon ecosystem?
6. What role do the Diego Ramírez Islands play in scientific monitoring near the Drake Passage?
7. How should Ruta Vicuña–Yendegaia be represented in relation to Tierra del Fuego and Parque Nacional Yendegaia?
8. Why is the Drake Passage considered the fundamental oceanic route between southern South America and Antarctica?
9. Which scientific bases are concentrated around Bahía Fildes, and how do they share the same logistical zone?
10. How do the Beagle Channel and the Strait of Magellan function as distinct maritime systems within Patagonia Austral?

## Known Corrections

- Ruta Vicuña–Yendegaia should not be modeled as part of Isla Navarino.
- Canal Beagle should not be modeled as a child of the Strait of Magellan.
- Antarctic entities should use careful terminology aligned with the Antarctic Treaty context.
