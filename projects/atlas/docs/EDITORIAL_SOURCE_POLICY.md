# Austral Beacon — Editorial Source Policy

## Purpose

This document defines the source hierarchy and editorial verification policy for Austral Beacon, End of the World Atlas, Antarctic Pulse, Antarctica Begins and related projects.

Its purpose is to prevent the ecosystem from repeating simplified or inaccurate claims from international media without checking Chilean, local, institutional and primary sources.

## Core Principle

For Chilean, Patagonian, Tierra del Fuego, Cape Horn and Antarctic gateway topics, prioritize Chilean official, local and institutional sources whenever possible.

Avoid repeating simplified claims from international media without verifying:

- administrative categories
- geographic categories
- territorial context
- institutional definitions
- local usage
- official recognition
- scientific or cartographic basis

## Source Priority

### Tier 1 — Official Chilean and Local Sources

Prefer these first when documenting Chilean southern and Antarctic entities:

- Gobierno de Chile
- Gobierno Regional de Magallanes
- Delegación Presidencial Provincial de la Antártica Chilena
- Municipalidad de Cabo de Hornos
- Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas (INE)
- Instituto Geográfico Militar (IGM)
- Servicio Hidrográfico y Oceanográfico de la Armada de Chile (SHOA)
- Armada de Chile
- DIRECTEMAR
- Instituto Antártico Chileno (INACH)
- Fuerza Aérea de Chile (FACH)
- CONAF
- Ministerio de Obras Públicas (MOP)
- Dirección General de Aeronáutica Civil (DGAC)

### Tier 2 — Local and Regional Knowledge Sources

Use these as supporting context:

- Local tourism organizations from Isla Navarino and Cabo de Hornos
- Regional Chilean media
- Local museums and cultural institutions
- Academic centers in Magallanes
- Centro Internacional Cabo de Hornos (CHIC)
- University and research publications connected to southern Chile

### Tier 3 — International Scientific and Antarctic Sources

Use for Antarctic, scientific and operational context:

- Antarctic Treaty Secretariat
- COMNAP
- SCAR
- NASA
- NOAA
- British Antarctic Survey
- Natural Earth
- GeoNames
- Wikidata
- OpenStreetMap

### Tier 4 — International Media and Generic Travel Sources

Use only with caution.

Do not repeat claims from international travel sites, blogs, videos or media unless verified against Tier 1, Tier 2 or Tier 3 sources.

## Editorial Rule: Puerto Williams and Puerto Toro

Always distinguish between:

- Puerto Williams: ciudad más austral del mundo.
- Puerto Toro: poblado or community located farther south, often described as the southernmost populated settlement/community.

Do not treat "city", "settlement", "village", "community" and "poblado" as equivalent terms.

When writing in English, clarify:

- Puerto Williams = southernmost city.
- Puerto Toro = southernmost village/community/settlement, depending on source wording.

## Editorial Rule: Antarctic Presence

Use careful Antarctic Treaty language.

Avoid:

- sensationalism
- territorial propaganda
- militarized framing
- unsupported geopolitical claims

Prefer:

- institutional context
- scientific activity
- logistics
- geography
- infrastructure
- Treaty-compatible language

## Verification Checklist

Before marking an Atlas entity as `reviewed` or `published`, verify:

- Coordinates
- Administrative category
- Geographic category
- Related entities
- Official Chilean source
- Local source when available
- Scientific or cartographic source when relevant
- No unsupported international-media claim
