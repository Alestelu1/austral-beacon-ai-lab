# Product overview

## Product

End of the World Travel Agent is a documentary travel assistant focused on the southernmost territories of Chile and Chilean Antarctic gateways.

## Primary users

- International and Chilean travelers researching southern Chile.
- Visitors planning routes through Punta Arenas, Puerto Natales, Puerto Williams, Isla Navarino and Cape Horn.
- Readers seeking cultural, geographic and connectivity context.

## User value

The assistant reduces fragmented research by combining destination knowledge, route explanations and source-backed guidance in one clear interface.

## MVP capabilities

- Answer destination questions.
- Explain how places connect.
- Recommend relevant internal content.
- Cite evidence and verification dates.
- State uncertainty instead of guessing.

## Editorial principles

- Documentary, concise and non-mass-market tone.
- Respect local communities, especially Yagán cultural heritage.
- Promote responsible tourism and environmental stewardship.
- Explain Chile's role as an Antarctic gateway without exaggeration.
- Mention cross-border context only when necessary for geographic or transport accuracy.

## Success criteria

- Responses are geographically accurate.
- Every material factual claim can be traced to a source.
- Users can distinguish verified facts, recommendations and uncertainty.
- The first vertical works reliably for Puerto Williams before expanding.
