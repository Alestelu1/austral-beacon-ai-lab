## Why this source matters

This document defines Chile's strategic Antarctic agenda for 2026–2030 and replaces the 2021–2025 plan. It is therefore a core policy source for Austral Beacon workflows involving Antarctic governance, logistics, infrastructure, science, tourism, regional development and connectivity.

## Five priority areas

1. Territorio Chileno Antártico: soberanía, presencia, infraestructura e identidad nacional.
2. Sistema del Tratado Antártico: liderazgo de Chile.
3. Ciencia antártica: fortalecimiento y nuevas fronteras.
4. Medioambiente antártico y sus ecosistemas: protección y conservación.
5. Región de Magallanes y de la Antártica Chilena: polo de desarrollo antártico.

## High-value findings for Austral Beacon

### Puerto Williams

The plan includes an action to strengthen Puerto Williams through an integrated Antarctic development plan for the 2026–2030 period. This makes Puerto Williams a first-class entity for the Atlas, Antarctica Begins, the Austral Connectivity Observatory and the Radar.

### Antarctic digital connectivity

The plan includes feasibility analysis for submarine cable routes connecting the Magallanes region with the Chilean Antarctic Territory, considering departure from Punta Arenas or Puerto Williams and service to permanent bases in the South Shetland Islands and Antarctic Peninsula.

This adds a fourth connectivity layer to the Austral Connectivity Observatory:

- air
- maritime
- terrestrial
- digital / telecommunications

### Sustainable Antarctic tourism

Objective No. 7 addresses sustainable economic activities allowed within the Antarctic Treaty System, with emphasis on controlled and sustainable tourism and biodiversity protection. The plan also includes studying Antarctic tourism development and its potential impacts.

### Infrastructure and logistics

Objective No. 10 focuses on sustainable Antarctic infrastructure and improved operational logistics capacity. This is relevant to future structured extraction of airfields, bases, stations, refuges, transport assets and logistics operators.
