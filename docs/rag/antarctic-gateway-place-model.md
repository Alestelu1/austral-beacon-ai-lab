# Antarctic Gateway Place & Infrastructure Model

## Purpose

This model connects canonical places, infrastructure, operators, products and policy commitments without collapsing planned projects into operational reality.

## Core chain

`city gateway -> Antarctic arrival node -> infrastructure -> operator capability -> product/service -> source`

The current verified gateway chain is centered on Punta Arenas and King George Island, while Puerto Williams is preserved as an explicit policy gateway and regional-development node.

## Canonical place entities

### Punta Arenas

Supported across institutional and first-party operator sources as a principal Chilean Antarctic gateway and departure/logistics node.

### Puerto Williams

MINREL's 2020 Antarctic tourism policy identifies Punta Arenas and Puerto Williams as gateway cities/bridges toward Antarctica. The 2026-2030 Plan repeats their gateway role and adds two strategic layers for Puerto Williams:

- candidate origin in Antarctic submarine-fiber feasibility analysis;
- explicit 2026-2030 task to strengthen the city through an integrated Antarctic development plan.

These are policy commitments/planning records, not proof that a cable or integrated development program has already been completed.

### King George Island / Isla Rey Jorge

Used in DAP and Antarctica21 first-party material as the Antarctic air-arrival/transfer node for operations from Punta Arenas.

### Aeródromo Teniente Marsh / Teniente Rodolfo Marsh Martin

The 2020 tourism policy treats permanent air connectivity through the Marsh airfield as a strength for Chilean Antarctic tourism. The 2026-2030 Plan contains infrastructure tasks for conservation of the movement area and additional aeronautical support improvements.

### Bahía Fildes

The 2026-2030 Plan contains planned MOP infrastructure for Bahía Fildes, including a pier and support apron for vessel activity, plus complementary support sheds for cargo/person transfer operations.

Status: **planned infrastructure**, not current operational capability unless independently verified.

### Villa Las Estrellas

DAP's 2026 Full Day briefing lists Villa Las Estrellas among the locations visited/considered during the King George Island program, subject to weather and operational conditions.

### Base Frei / Frei Station

The name appears in DAP logistics material and the Antarctica21 2027-2028 brochure. This record is intentionally minimal. Institutional attributes, ownership, facilities and exact relationships should be enriched later from FACH/INACH sources.

## Graph rules

1. **No unsourced geography:** do not infer containment or adjacency only from commercial maps.
2. **Plan != operation:** planned infrastructure has a separate node/type from operational assets.
3. **Commercial freshness:** operator routes/products require season-aware revalidation.
4. **Authority scope:** operator material is authoritative for the operator's own products/capabilities; institutional material is preferred for policy, infrastructure programs and public roles.
5. **One canonical ID per entity:** future documents should link to these IDs instead of recreating duplicate place records.

## Next enrichment targets

- Institutional geography for King George Island, Bahía Fildes and station relationships.
- Puerto Williams infrastructure and connectivity records from official regional/national sources.
