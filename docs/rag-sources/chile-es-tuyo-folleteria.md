## Editorial Notes

This source should be treated as an official Chilean tourism reference, especially for travel planning, destination framing, regional tourism context, brochures and maps.

For technical, geographic, administrative or scientific claims, verify with more specific Chilean sources when available, such as:

- INE Chile.
- Armada de Chile.
- INACH.
- CONAF.
- Instituto Geográfico Militar.
- Municipalidad de Cabo de Hornos.
- Gobierno Regional de Magallanes.
- Universities or regional research institutions.
