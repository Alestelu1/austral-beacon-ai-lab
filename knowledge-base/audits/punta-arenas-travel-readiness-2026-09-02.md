## Antarctic-context guard

The phrase “base estratégica para la exploración antártica” is preserved as an attributed Chile Travel/SERNATUR statement. It does not independently establish:

- principal or leading gateway status;
- superiority over other gateways;
- aggregate Antarctic passenger traffic;
- market share or gateway share;
- current expedition departures;
- current cruise availability;
- current commercial operating conditions.

Aggregate Antarctic traffic figures remain blocked until an authoritative Chilean statistical source is identified and verified.

## Operational content blocked from stable Travel projection

- current flights and schedules;
- seasonal international routes;
- current ferry schedules;
- current expedition departures;
- current cruise availability;
- current airport or port operating conditions;
- weather;
- current visitor services;
- prices, fares or seat availability;
- unverified coordinates.

These require current verification and must not be inferred from stable identity or infrastructure claims.

## Source review

Primary Chilean institutional evidence used in the canonical package includes:

- SUBDERE — regional administrative identity and regional capital;
- Chile Travel / SERNATUR — city / Strait context and attributed Antarctic exploration framing;
- Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas — urban/geographic support;
- Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional — Comuna de Punta Arenas administrative context;
- Municipalidad de Punta Arenas / Ministerio del Medio Ambiente — official communal planning evidence identifying the Punta Arenas urban area within the commune.

## Phase 3E constraints

The preview should be a city page, not a live transport dashboard or Antarctic market page. It should present stable city identity, administrative context, Strait relationship and the attributed Antarctic context. Production routes and previous previews must remain unchanged. The Travel Assistant should remain out of scope.
