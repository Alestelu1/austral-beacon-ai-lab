# Estrecho de Magallanes — Travel readiness audit

Date: 2026-09-02
Entity: `strait-of-magellan`
Projection: `knowledge-base/projections/travel/strait-of-magellan-v1.json`
Result: **READY FOR WEB PREVIEW WITH EXISTING GUARDS**

## Scope

This audit checks whether the stable public Travel projection is sufficiently grounded for a documentary destination/geography preview. It does not authorize live navigation advice, treaty interpretation, geopolitical argumentation or operational claims.

## Verified stable claims

The following approved claims were re-checked against current Chilean institutional material from DIRECTEMAR / Armada de Chile:

1. `strait-geographic-setting` — supported. DIRECTEMAR describes the Strait as a passage in the southern extreme of South America, between Chilean Patagonia, Isla Grande de Tierra del Fuego and the island system to its west toward the Pacific.
2. `strait-jurisdiction-chile` — supported as a descriptive institutional statement. DIRECTEMAR states that the Strait lies entirely within Chilean jurisdiction and within the Región de Magallanes y de la Antártica Chilena. Travel must not extend this into treaty interpretation, disputed-boundary reasoning, legal conclusions or geopolitical advocacy.
3. `strait-length-330-nm` — supported. DIRECTEMAR defines the Strait from Punta Dúngenes at its eastern mouth to Islotes Evangelistas at its western mouth, with a total length of 330 nautical miles.

## Conditionally allowed stable descriptions

Re-checked against DIRECTEMAR:

- `first-narrows-length` — Primera Angostura: approximately 8 nautical miles, Punta Delgada to Bajo Satélite.
- `second-narrows-length` — Segunda Angostura: approximately 12 nautical miles, Cabo Gregorio to Cabo San Vicente.
- `paso-tortuoso-length` — Paso Tortuoso: approximately 6.3 nautical miles, Punta San Jerónimo to Cabo Quod.

These may be rendered only as stable geographic descriptions. They must not be combined with current navigation, current/tide, ferry, pilotage or traffic-control advice.

## Readiness decision

`travel-strait-of-magellan-v1` is suitable for a **preview-only, noindex/nofollow web migration** provided the website:

- resolves only projection-approved claims and relationships;
- preserves claim IDs, source IDs and verification metadata;
- treats navigation and crossing questions as requiring current authoritative verification;
- does not infer deferred targets such as Punta Dúngenes, Islotes Evangelistas, Primera Angostura, Segunda Angostura, Paso Tortuoso, Cabo Froward or heritage/history entities as canonical website relationships;
- does not publish coordinates until authoritative canonical geometry exists;
- does not expand descriptive Chilean jurisdiction context into legal, treaty or geopolitical interpretation.

## Follow-up work not required for Phase 3D

History, heritage, Indigenous context, hydrography/cartography depth and infrastructure can be expanded later through dedicated canonical review. Their incompleteness does not block a narrow stable geographic Travel preview.
