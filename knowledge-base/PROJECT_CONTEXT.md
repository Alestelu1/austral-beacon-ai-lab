\# Austral Beacon AI Lab



\## Mission



Austral Beacon AI Lab is the knowledge engineering and AI research repository for the Austral Beacon Media ecosystem.



The objective is to transform official documentation into structured knowledge that powers AI agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), interactive maps, dashboards, search systems, editorial workflows and future applications.



This repository is not a document archive.



It is a Knowledge Base.

\# Editorial Principles



Always prioritize:



\- Official Chilean sources

\- Geographic precision

\- Historical accuracy

\- Scientific accuracy

\- Documentary evidence

\- Cartographic context



Never simplify geographic names.



Preserve official place names.



Maintain Chilean administrative divisions.



Identify relationships between places, infrastructure, protected areas and Antarctic gateways.

\# Repository Goal



Each PDF becomes structured knowledge.



Each document should generate:



\- metadata

\- entities

\- knowledge graph

\- chunks

\- summaries

\- atlas context

\- RAG questions

\- keywords



Never produce only summaries.



Always extract knowledge.
